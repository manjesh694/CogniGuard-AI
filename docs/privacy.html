Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 | Effective Immediately

At CogniGuard AI ("we", "our", or "us"), we believe privacy and security are fundamental human rights. This Privacy Policy describes how the CogniGuard AI Chrome Extension and web services handle your data with a strict Zero-Knowledge & Privacy-First Architecture.

🛡️ Our Zero-Data Guarantee

CogniGuard AI does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or harvest your personal browsing history, passwords, or personal documents. All security analysis is performed locally on your device or in disposable memory sandboxes.

1. Information We Process

To protect your device against infostealer trojans and malicious payloads, CogniGuard AI analyzes the following data strictly for security verification:

  • Download Metadata: File name, file extension, and source URL of initiated downloads to verify whether executable code is disguised inside documents.
  • Threat Incident Counters: Local numerical tallies of scanned and paused files stored solely inside your browser's local sandbox (chrome.storage.local).

2. Chrome Web Store Permissions Justification

CogniGuard AI requests only the minimal permissions required to provide real-time protection:

  • downloads: Required to pause untrusted executable downloads in memory before they write to your physical hard drive.
  • storage: Required to locally store security metrics, rate-limits, and user whitelist preferences on your machine.
  • webNavigation: Required to detect and intercept deceptive phishing domains and fake login redirects.
  • notifications: Required to display native Windows/macOS desktop alerts when an unauthorized intrusion is blocked.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers under any circumstances.
